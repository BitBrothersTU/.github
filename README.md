# Bit Brothers at Towson University

Bit Brothers is a student technology community where young men of color can empower and encourage one another throughout their technology journeys.

Our mission is to amplify the voices of men of color in technology, support their retention in technology-related programs, and connect members with the resources needed to achieve their personal, professional, and educational goals.

## What We Do

* Build collaborative, real-world technology projects
* Develop technical and professional skills
* Connect students with mentors and industry professionals
* Host workshops, meetings, and community events
* Create opportunities for leadership and career development
* Support and celebrate men of color in technology
